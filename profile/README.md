## LLMs in HCI Data Work: Bridging the Gap Between Information Retrieval and Responsible Research Practices

Extracting and analyzing data from scientific papers is critical for advancing human-computer interaction (HCI) research. Our project investigates the potential of Large Language Models (LLMs) for efficient and accurate information retrieval in HCI studies. We implemented and evaluated an information retrieval system leveraging state-of-the-art LLMs, specifically GPT-3.5 and Llama-2, to extract experimental details from HCI literature. Our results indicate that these models achieve high accuracy in information extraction while highlighting challenges in handling multi-stage experimental parameters and structured datasets.

We designed a system to facilitate information retrieval from scientific papers, integrating structured text analysis techniques. This repository includes our dataset, evaluation results, and methodological framework.
